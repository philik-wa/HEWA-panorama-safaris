01 — Narok County

The Quiet Mara

Big cats, open plains and — from July to October — the Great Migration crossing the Mara River. These four camps span the full range of comfort and price, all within reach of the same game-rich territory.

Budget-Friendly

Mara Riverside Camp

Simple en-suite tents, shared campfire and communal dining. Great value for first-timers.

$95Per Person, Per Night
Mid-Range Comfort

Sekenani Ridge Lodge

Comfortable cottages with a pool and game-view dining, a short drive from the reserve gate.

$220Per Person, Per Night
Luxury

Enkoyanai Luxury Tented Camp

Private plunge pools, in-tent butler service and prime positioning on the migration route.

$480Per Person, Per Night
Ultra-Luxury

Kichaka Private Reserve House

A full private villa buyout with a dedicated guide, vehicle and in-suite spa treatments.

$950Per Person, Per Night
02 — Kajiado County

Mount Kenya

Kenya's largest elephant herds, photographed against the snowcap of Kilimanjaro. A calmer, quieter alternative to the Mara.

Budget-Friendly

Kimana Gate Bandas

Simple stone bandas just outside the park gate — no-frills, close to the action.

$85Per Person, Per Night
Mid-Range Comfort

Kilimanjaro View Lodge

Comfortable rooms facing the mountain, with a pool and a relaxed, family-friendly pace.

$200Per Person, Per Night
Luxury

Amboseli Under-the-Volcano Camp

Tented suites with unobstructed Kilimanjaro views and private sundowner decks.

$430Per Person, Per Night
Ultra-Luxury

Ol Tukai Private Villas

Standalone villas with private plunge pools, a personal chef and a fly-in airstrip transfer.

$880Per Person, Per Night

Ol Pejeta Conservancy — The Rhinos' Abode

See black and white rhino in their natural habitat, alongside conservation work you can visit in person.

Budget-Friendly

Sweetwaters Public Campsite

$80Per Person, Per Night

Pejeta Ridge Eco-Lodge

Solar-powered cottages with conservancy game drives and rhino tracking included.

$240Per Person, Per Night

Kicheche Laikipia Camp

Small, six-tent luxury camp with guided walking safaris and horseback options.

$500Per Person, Per Night
Ultra-Luxury

Solio Private Rhino Sanctuary Villa

A private villa inside a rhino sanctuary with dedicated anti-poaching-unit ride-alongs.

$1,050Per Person, Per Night

Mombasa, Kilifi & The Coast

Warm water, dhow sailing, Old Town Mombasa and slow dinners on the sand — the classic way to close out a safari.

Budget-Friendly

Watamu Private Island Retreat
