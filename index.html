About Us

Built by guides,
not a booking engine.

Hewa Safaris started in 2012 with one Land Cruiser and a handful of camps we trusted. We're still small on purpose: every trip is planned by a guide who has driven the route themselves, and every camp on our list has been visited, not just listed.

"Hewa" means air, or spirit, in Swahili — the feeling we try to hand you the moment the plains open up in front of the vehicle.
